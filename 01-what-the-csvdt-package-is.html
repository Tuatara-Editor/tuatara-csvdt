What the csvdt package is

The package that runs the csvdt program over the records of a CSV buffer.

csvdt converts the timestamps and durations in the records of a CSV file. The package hands it whole records from the buffer, in the background, and shows what csvdt writes in a buffer of its own, *csvdt output*. The buffer the records came from is never changed.

csvdt is a program of its own, installed apart from Tuatara. Its options and what they do are in its own help; C-c v h shows it. This manual covers what the package adds: which records are sent, how the arguments are typed, and what happens to the result.

1.1 The keys

In a CSV or TSV (tab-separated values) buffer, the keys are under C-c v. Tuatara labels the prefix csvdt in its key help.

KeyCommandWhat it runs csvdt over
C-c v rcsvdt-run-dwimbanked lines and selection, else all
C-c v bcsvdt-run-bufferthe whole buffer
C-c v SPCcsvdt-run-regionthe selection
C-c v kcsvdt-run-bankedthe banked lines
C-c v ?csvdt-describe-runnothing: says what a run would cover
C-c v hcsvdt-helpnothing: shows csvdt's --help
C-c v vcsvdt-versionnothing: says which csvdt runs

In any other buffer the commands are run with M-x, or from a key of your own in the init file:

(bind-key "; c" 'csvdt-run-dwim :map :leader)   ; SPC ; c

1.2 If csvdt is not found

M-x check-health says whether Tuatara finds csvdt. When it does not, chapter 5 says how to name its path.
